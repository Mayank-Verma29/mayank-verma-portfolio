Customer Behaviour Analysis.

Exploring shopping patterns, customer segments, subscription behaviour, product preferences and purchase trends through SQL analysis and an interactive Power BI dashboard.

01 / PROJECT OVERVIEW

Understanding shopping behaviour

This project analyses customer shopping records to explore purchase amounts, product categories, subscription status, gender, age groups, shipping preferences, discounts and review ratings.

02 / BUSINESS QUESTIONS

Questions explored

  • How does spending compare by gender?
  • Do subscribers spend more than non-subscribers?
  • Which products receive the highest average ratings?
  • How do shipping types compare by average purchase amount?
  • Which products and categories contribute most to sales?
  • Are repeat buyers more likely to subscribe?

05 / SQL ANALYSIS

Questions answered with SQL.

The SQL script investigates gender-based revenue, discount users spending above average, top-rated products, Express versus Standard shipping, subscriber spending, discount rates by product, customer segmentation, top products within each category, repeat-buyer subscription patterns and revenue by age group.

07 / BUSINESS CONSIDERATIONS

How the analysis could be used.

  • Review category-level performance when planning inventory and promotional campaigns.
  • Compare subscriber and non-subscriber purchasing patterns before designing subscription benefits.
  • Investigate product-level review ratings alongside sales to identify customer experience opportunities.
  • Use demographic filters to explore patterns without assuming behaviour based on demographics alone.
  • Check missing review-rating values before using ratings in further analysis.
