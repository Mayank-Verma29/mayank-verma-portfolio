BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE / CASE STUDY 01

Amazon Sales Analysis.

Analysing e-commerce sales data to understand revenue performance, customer spending, product contribution and order status, then presenting findings in Power BI.

MySQLPower BISQLData Analysis

Power BI dashboard created for this project.

01 / OVERVIEW

Understanding sales performance

This project explores a sample e-commerce dataset containing 250 order records and 12 fields. The analysis focuses on revenue, quantity, customer contribution, product and category performance, locations and order status.

02 / BUSINESS QUESTIONS

What does the data tell us?

  • How much revenue and quantity were recorded?
  • Which locations, categories and products contributed most?
  • Who were the highest-spending customers?
  • How were orders distributed across statuses?
  • How did revenue vary by month?

03 / DATASET SNAPSHOT

The dataset at a glance.

Fields include order ID, date, product, category, price, quantity, total sales, customer name, customer location, payment method, status and location. No missing values were found in the supplied CSV.

$81,000

Highest location revenue

New York had the highest total sales among listed locations.

Top product revenue

88 / 85 / 77

Order status counts

Completed, Pending and Cancelled orders respectively.

These findings describe the supplied sample project dataset, not actual Amazon company-wide performance.

07 / RECOMMENDATIONS

Possible business actions.

  • Review inventory planning for high-revenue categories such as Electronics and Home Appliances.
  • Investigate reasons behind the 77 cancelled orders and identify ways to reduce cancellations.
  • Study purchasing patterns of high-spending customers to inform retention initiatives.
  • Explore lower sales of Books, T-Shirts and Jeans before making pricing or promotion decisions.
  • Compare results over a longer period before drawing conclusions about seasonality or growth.
