MACHINE LEARNING / CASE STUDY 04

Product Recommendation System.

A product-to-product recommendation prototype built from e-commerce order history. The notebook transforms customer purchases into an interaction matrix, measures product similarity with cosine similarity and prepares a Streamlit interface for exploring recommendations.

PythonPandasNumPySciPyScikit-learnStreamlit

01 / PROJECT OVERVIEW

Help shoppers discover related products.

The project uses the Olist e-commerce dataset. Orders, order items and product metadata are combined to create customer–product purchase interactions. Products with similar customer purchase patterns are compared to generate product recommendations.

02 / WORKFLOW

From raw orders to recommendations.

01 · LoadOrders, order items and products
02 · MergeJoin on order and product IDs
03 · PrepareClean purchase pairs and filter rare activity
04 · CompareSparse matrix + cosine similarity
05 · RecommendRank similar products for selection

03 / DATA PREPARATION

Build purchase interactions.

  • Load orders, order items and product metadata.
  • Merge orders with order items using order_id.
  • Merge product details using product_id.
  • Keep customer and product identifiers, remove missing and duplicate pairs.
  • Create a binary purchase = 1 interaction indicator.
  • Filter products with fewer than 10 purchases and customers with fewer than 3 purchases.

04 / RECOMMENDATION METHOD

Item-based collaborative filtering.

  • Encode customer and product IDs as category codes.
  • Build a SciPy CSR sparse customer–product matrix.
  • Transpose the matrix so products become rows.
  • Calculate product-to-product cosine similarity.
  • Sort similarity scores and select the five nearest products, excluding the selected product itself.
  • Translate product category labels from Portuguese to English for display.

06 / EXPLORATION & OUTPUTS

Inspecting similarity and product patterns.

01

Product similarity heatmap

A heatmap is created from a 10-by-10 slice of the product similarity matrix to inspect similarity values between products.

02

Frequently purchased products

The notebook sums purchase interactions by product and plots the ten highest counts. The displayed output includes garden tools, bed-bath-table and watches-gifts categories.

03

Top-five similar products

The recommendation function sorts the selected product's similarity scores and returns up to five related product categories.

04

Streamlit interface

The notebook generates a Streamlit app with a product selector and a Recommend button to display recommendations.

07 / IMPLEMENTATION NOTE

Current prototype scope.

The notebook output shows 102,425 distinct customer–product purchase pairs after the initial merge and cleaning, followed by a much smaller filtered interaction set of 114 rows after applying the minimum-purchase thresholds. The Streamlit code shown in the notebook displays English product-category labels as selectable items and recommendations, rather than human-readable product names or SKU-level product cards.

Transparency: This is a learning/prototype project based on purchase co-occurrence patterns. The supplied notebook does not report offline recommendation metrics (such as Precision@K, Recall@K or MAP@K), a train/test evaluation, or measured business impact. These are sensible next improvements, not results claimed by this project.

08 / NEXT IMPROVEMENTS

How this could be strengthened.

  • Retain product IDs alongside readable product names and category labels in the app.
  • Review the filtering thresholds and resulting 114-row interaction set to ensure sufficient coverage.
  • Evaluate recommendations using a held-out interaction set and ranking metrics such as Precision@K and Recall@K.
  • Compare item-based collaborative filtering with a popularity baseline and category-based recommendations.
  • Persist and load the similarity artifact and product lookup table as reproducible app assets.
